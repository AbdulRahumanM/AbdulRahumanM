## Notes for practice-manager outreach

Practice managers are often the gatekeeper and should usually be contacted
before the physician. Same message works with light edits:

- Open with "I'm hoping to get 30–60 minutes with one of your physicians,
  and wanted to check with you first."
- Emphasize the honorarium/early-access angle more — managers are weighing
  disruption to the physician's day against the ask.
- Ask directly: "Is there a physician on your team who might be a good fit,
  and what's the best way to reach them?"
