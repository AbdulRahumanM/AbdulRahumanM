What this is

I’m Abdul, and this is my own research. There is no company behind this page, and I’m not asking you to look at or buy a product. I want to understand how your work actually runs — caseload, documentation, coordination, tools and constraints — so I learn from practice rather than guessing.

If you are a practising APD in Australia, I would be grateful to hear how your day really works. Wave 1 is focused on Sydney and NSW. A conversation is useful on its own; shadowing is optional and only happens later if it can be arranged properly.

Who I’m looking for

I’m hoping to speak with practising Accredited Practising Dietitians, starting in Sydney and elsewhere in NSW, including:

  • solo private-practice owners
  • group private-practice owners
  • dietitians in hospital or acute settings
  • dietitians working in aged care or NDIS
  • both new graduates (under 2 years practising as an APD) and experienced APDs (2+ years practising as an APD)

New graduates are in scope. You do not need to agree to shadowing to have a conversation. If you practise elsewhere in Australia, I may be in touch in a later wave unless a first-wave conversation is clearly a good fit.

What’s involved

A conversation (the main invitation)

About 30–45 minutes, by video, phone, or in person in Sydney, about how your work runs day to day. We can go into whatever is most real for you — caseload, documentation, coordination, funding such as Medicare CDM, NDIS, private health or aged care, and the way the day is organised. There is no script you have to sell to, and no product to review.

Shadowing (optional, later)

If it can be arranged with the appropriate workplace or practice approvals, and with patient consent where anyone else’s care could be observed, I may later ask about an observational shadowing session of 2–4 hours. That is a separate step. Saying no to shadowing is completely fine, and it does not affect the conversation.

Recording is optional and entirely up to you. I will only record and transcribe a session if you opt in. We can decide at the start, and saying no is completely fine. We can still have a useful conversation from notes alone.

What you get

As a thank-you, I can share a short summary of what I learn across these conversations. There is no cash payment and no gift card. This is a one-time research conversation (and, only if you later agree and approvals are in place, a possible shadowing visit), not the start of a sales relationship.

Privacy and consent

This research runs under my name, Abdul, in Sydney. I intend to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

  • I will record and transcribe a conversation only if you opt in. You can say no and we can work from notes.
  • I will not collect patient-identifiable information. I am interested in workflow and process — how work gets done — not the content of patient records or a patient’s story.
  • Shadowing proceeds only with permission from the practice or workplace, and with patient consent where a patient’s care could be observed.
  • Notes from our conversation are used for this research. In the research record they are kept against a participant ID rather than as a named file shared with others. I will not publish your name or practice-identifying details without your agreement.
  • If you would like to know what I hold, have it corrected, or have it deleted, email me and I will deal with that. I will also tell you, if you ask, how long I expect to keep the notes.

If it helps your practice, I am happy to sign a confidentiality or research agreement before we meet — just ask.
